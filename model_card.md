## Reflection and Ethics

Overall, there are various limiations and biases. One such example is that the AI planner is rule-based. Because of this, it may be difficult for the system to handle complex, ambiguous, or unusual user goals.

The system could also be misused if users rely on it too heavily without taking their pet’s specific health needs or circumstances into consideration. With this in mind, the user should always review tasks before acting on them.

During testing, it was surprising to see how many variations need to be tested because even one small change could lead to a diffferent output 

Throughout development, AI tools were used to assist with debugging, design decisions, and improving code. A beneficial suggestion was to separate the planning, validation, and scheduling logic into different components. However, a flawed suggestion was assuming that all task frequencies would behave consistently across the system. This led to a mismatch where "bi-weekly" was accepted in the interface but not fully supported in the scheduling logic.
